After 10+ years of building software, I've learned one thing: **the faster you ship, the faster you learn**.

## The Problem With Slow Development

Most projects fail not because of bad code, but because they took too long to reach users. By the time they launched:

- The market had moved on
- The budget ran out
- The team lost momentum

## My Approach: Design, Build, Ship

I've refined a process that gets working products in front of users quickly:

### 1. Ruthless Prioritization

Not every feature needs to be in v1. I work with clients to identify the **one thing** their product must do well.

### 2. Modern Tools, Fast Results

I use tools that accelerate development without sacrificing quality:

- **Next.js** for instant static sites and server-side rendering
- **React Native** for cross-platform mobile apps
- **Claude Code** for AI-assisted development
- **Vercel/Coolify** for instant deployments

### 3. Design and Code Together

I don't hand off designs to developers - I am both. This eliminates the back-and-forth that kills momentum.

## The Result

Products that would take agencies 3-6 months, I deliver in weeks. Not because I cut corners, but because I've eliminated everything that doesn't add value.
