## Speed is the feature

When I say "fast," I don't mean fast delivery (though I do that too). I mean fast websites.

Google measures your website speed with Core Web Vitals. Sites that load slowly get penalized in search rankings. Most small business websites score poorly because they're built on bloated platforms with dozens of plugins.

I build with **Next.js** — the same framework used by Nike, Netflix, and Notion. Your small business website runs on enterprise-grade technology:

- **Sub-second load times** — Pages load before your visitor finishes blinking
- **Server-side rendering** — Content is ready when Google crawls it
- **Automatic image optimization** — Photos load at the right size for each device
- **Edge deployment** — Your site loads fast from anywhere in the world

Why does this matter for a small business? Because 53% of mobile visitors leave a site that takes longer than 3 seconds to load. Every slow second costs you customers.

## SEO is baked in, not bolted on

Most web agencies build your site first, then "add SEO" as an afterthought. Maybe they install a plugin and call it done.

That's backwards. At GEXP, SEO is part of the architecture:

### Technical SEO from line one

```
✓ Semantic HTML structure (h1, h2, h3 hierarchy)
✓ Meta tags generated dynamically for every page
✓ Structured data (JSON-LD) — tells Google exactly what your business does
✓ XML sitemap — auto-generated and submitted
✓ Canonical URLs — no duplicate content issues
✓ Mobile-first responsive design
✓ Core Web Vitals optimized
```

### Local SEO built into the foundation

For local businesses, I add:

- **LocalBusiness schema** — Google understands your location, hours, services
- **Google Business Profile integration** — Your website and Maps listing work together
- **Location-specific content** — Pages optimized for "[service] in [your city]"
- **NAP consistency** — Name, Address, Phone number identical everywhere

### Content that ranks

I help structure your content so it answers the questions your customers actually search for. Not keyword stuffing — genuine, useful content that Google rewards.

## Lead capture that actually converts

A website without lead capture is a billboard on a highway. People see it, maybe remember it, probably forget it.

Every GEXP website includes:

- **Strategic CTAs** — "Book a Call," "Get a Quote," "Send Message" placed where visitors are most likely to act
- **WhatsApp integration** — One tap to message you directly (critical for Latin American markets)
- **Contact forms** that actually work — emails land in your inbox, not spam
- **Booking links** — Cal.com or similar integration for scheduling

No complicated funnels. No 47-step forms. Just clear paths from "I'm interested" to "I'm a customer."
