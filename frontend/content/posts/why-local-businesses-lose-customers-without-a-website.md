## You're invisible to everyone who doesn't already follow you

Here's a scenario I see constantly: a restaurant owner in Pérez Zeledón posts beautiful food photos on Instagram. Gets likes, comments, shares. Business feels like it's growing.

Then someone searches "best restaurant near me" on Google. That restaurant doesn't show up. The one with a basic website and Google Business profile does.

That's the gap. Social media reaches people who already know you exist. A website reaches people who are actively looking for what you sell.

## Social media is rented land

I've had this conversation with dozens of local entrepreneurs across Costa Rica. They tell me "I don't need a website, I have Facebook." Then I ask:

- **What happens when the algorithm changes?** Facebook organic reach dropped from 16% to under 2% in the last few years. Your posts reach almost nobody unless you pay.
- **What happens if your account gets hacked or banned?** I've seen businesses lose thousands of followers overnight with no recourse.
- **Can customers find you at 2 AM when they're planning tomorrow?** A website works 24/7. Your social media story disappears in 24 hours.

You don't own your Instagram page. Meta does. You don't own your Facebook audience. The algorithm does.

A website is yours. Your domain, your content, your leads.

## What a website actually does for a local business

Let me break it down with real impact:

### 1. You show up when people search

When someone types "plumber in San José" or "yoga classes Escazú," Google looks for websites. Not Instagram profiles. Not Facebook pages. Websites with proper SEO, location data, and relevant content.

### 2. You look legitimate

Like it or not, people judge businesses by their online presence. A professional website says "this is a real business." A Facebook-only presence says "this might be a side hustle."

### 3. You capture leads automatically

A website with a contact form, WhatsApp button, or booking system captures potential customers while you sleep. No manually responding to DMs at midnight.

### 4. You control the narrative

On social media, your content competes with memes, reels, and your competitor's ads. On your website, the visitor's full attention is on your business, your services, your call to action.

### 5. You build a long-term asset

Every blog post, every service page, every customer testimonial on your website builds SEO authority over time. It compounds. Social media posts get buried in 48 hours.

## Start simple

You don't need a 20-page website. You need:

1. **A homepage** that clearly says what you do and who you serve
2. **A services/menu page** with details and pricing
3. **A contact page** with a form, phone number, WhatsApp link, and your location
4. **Google Business Profile** connected to your website

That's it. Four pages. Build from there.
